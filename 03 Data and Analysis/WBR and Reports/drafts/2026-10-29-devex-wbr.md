## Experience

Source: Snowflake `PRODUCTION.TRANSFORMED_DATA.PRODUCT_API_REQUESTS_DAILY`, query `01c80e75-0520-77dc-001b-b50212cbedaf` for the distinct rows and `01c80e75-0520-77dc-001b-b50212cbe71b` for the family rows. Rules: definitions v1. Clean team accounts. Internal callers, Kubernetes controllers, and registry clients removed. An account in two families is counted once in the first row and once in each family row.

**What:** Distinct accounts on doctl, Terraform, an official SDK, or MCP went from 24,015 in the week of Oct 15–21 to 24,295 in the week of Oct 22–28 (+280, +1.2%). doctl went from 18,309 to 18,551 (+242, +1.3%). Remote MCP went from 2,932 to 3,029 (+97, +3.3%). godo went from 1,595 to 1,482 (−113, −7.1%). pydo went from 304 to 288 (−16, −5.3%). Terraform was flat (2,887 to 2,889).

**Why:** The headline did not change shape. doctl is still most of the distinct accounts, and its gain is about the size of the headline gain. The move that needs a cause is godo. A 7% drop in one week, while doctl and remote MCP rose, is not a missing day in the table. The cause is unexplained. curl rose from 9,100 to 9,481 (+4.2%) and is not in the headline; that is a separate path, not an explanation of the godo drop.

**New this week:** No definition change. The daily table was not checked against the raw log for these two weeks, so the counts stay directional. Nothing shipped to a developer.
