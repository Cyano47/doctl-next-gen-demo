# Books

A reference library for the PDFs in `~/Downloads/Books`. 141 files, 86 unique works after exact duplicates. Notes are grouped by the decision they change. A book that repeats another book lives in that book’s theme, not in its own file.

Read the theme when you are about to make the call. The source map at the bottom is only there to find which file holds a title.

## How disagreements are handled

Where two books give different instructions, the theme states both and says when to follow each. The full argument is in one file. Other themes link to it instead of restating it.

| Decision | File |
| --- | --- |
| What customers can tell you, and what you still have to test | [What to build](what-to-build.md) |
| MVP, pretotype, or prototype | [What to build](what-to-build.md) |
| Weekly discovery or a five-day sprint | [What to build](what-to-build.md) |

| A secret you commit to, or a test you run | [Where to play](where-to-play.md) |
| Niche, new category, or a wider market | [Where to play](where-to-play.md) |
| A goal and a strategy | [Where to play](where-to-play.md) |
| What the PM job actually is | [How the team works](how-the-team-works.md) |
| Roadmaps, OKRs, and a North Star | [How the team works](how-the-team-works.md) |

| Habits, points, and nudges | [Getting adopted](getting-adopted.md) |
| Whether to trust a study, a metric, or an outcome | [Judgment](judgment.md) |
| The bargain on paper, and the conversation in the room | [The person and the room](the-person-and-the-room.md) |

## Themes

1. [What to build](what-to-build.md) — discovery, jobs, experiments, whether the product explains itself.
2. [Where to play](where-to-play.md) — strategy, market choice, category, disruption.
3. [How the team works](how-the-team-works.md) — the role, the roadmap, goals, ops, and what interviews reward.
4. [Getting adopted](getting-adopted.md) — channels, networks, habits, launch, go-to-market motions.
5. [Judgment](judgment.md) — biases, bets, measurement, and when more data is noise.
6. [The person and the room](the-person-and-the-room.md) — managing, negotiating, writing, candor.
7. [APIs](apis.md) — the two primers, which agree.
